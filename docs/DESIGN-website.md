## Brand & Style

The design system embodies the grounded reliability of an established educational supplier, merged with the clarity of modern institutional procurement. Serving school principals, bursars, government curriculum evaluators, and discerning parents across Western Kenya and Nairobi, the interface prioritizes immediate legitimacy, high-density clarity, and warm academic reassurance.

The visual direction blends **Corporate / Modern** precision with **Tactile Educational Warmth**. It eschews sterile minimalism in favor of structured data presentation, clear institutional signifiers (KRA verification stamps, CBC/JSS accreditation tags), warm ochre and terracotta highlights derived from natural notebook covers and school clay, and oxford navy anchors that convey fiscal stability.

## Colors

The color palette bridges traditional institutional dignity with warm retail accessibility:

- **Primary (`#C25608`, Ochre Ember / Amber Terracotta):** Used for focal calls to action, active navigation indicators, key promotional badges, and brand identity marks. It reflects energetic African soil, quality bookbinding, and active learning.
- **Secondary (`#0F172A`, Oxford Navy):** Anchor color used for headers, primary typography, deep high-contrast navigation bands, enterprise pricing matrices, and formal institutional notices.
- **Tertiary (`#0284C7`, Academic Azure):** Reserved for institutional certifications, CBC/JSS curricular approval tags, KRA tax compliance signifiers, and data downloads (PDF price catalogues).
- **Neutral / Canvas (`#FDFCF9`, Ivory Linen):** A soft, eye-resting warm off-white inspired by high-grade bankbook paper and printed textbook leaves. Prevents glare when processing large tabular orders. A clean secondary surface neutral (`#F1F5F9`) serves table alternating rows and card backgrounds.

## Typography

The pairing combines the technical, structured authority of **Space Grotesk** for headlines, tabular codes, and official badges with the humanist warmth and readability of **Plus Jakarta Sans** for body copy and continuous inventory descriptions.

- Headings demand decisive clarity without appearing frivolous, matching institutional procurement requirements.
- Space Grotesk’s subtle geometric quirks give the catalog, SKU numbers, and branch identifiers an engineered, dependable tone.
- Body copy remains open, legible in high temperatures and mobile browser environments across varying device formats.

## Layout & Spacing

This design system uses a strict **12-column responsive fluid grid** capped at a maximum desktop width of `1280px` to maintain optimal line-lengths and data-table cohesion.

- **Desktop (>= 1024px):** 12 columns, `1.25rem` (20px) gutters, `2.5rem` (40px) outer margins. Multi-column category grids, institutional quotation sidebars, and side-by-side branch hours.
- **Tablet (768px - 1023px):** 8 columns, `1rem` (16px) gutters, `1.5rem` (24px) outer margins. Inventory tables retain horizontal scroll wrappers with pinned product headers.
- **Mobile (< 768px):** 4 columns, `0.75rem` (12px) gutters, `1rem` (16px) outer margins. Catalog cards transition into streamlined stacked list items for quick finger navigation.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy employs **Low-Contrast Structural Outlines** reinforced by **Tonal Surface Layers** rather than heavy blurred drop shadows. This preserves crispness in high-sunlight mobile viewing and matches professional printed stationery:

- **Level 0 (Flat/Base):** Neutral background `#FDFCF9`.
- **Level 1 (Card/Container):** Pure white `#FFFFFF` surface bordered with `1px solid #E2E8F0` and an ultra-subtle tinted grounding shadow: `0 1px 3px 0 rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.05)`.
- **Level 2 (Hover / Active Cards / Branch Panels):** `#FFFFFF` surface lifted with `0 4px 12px -2px rgba(194, 86, 8, 0.08), 0 2px 6px -1px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.04)` and border transitioning to `#CBD5E1`.
- **Level 3 (Modals / Cart / Institutional Quotation Drawers):** `0 20px 25px -5px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.12), 0 8px 10px -6px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.06)`, framed by a `#0F172A` hairline border.

## Shapes

The shape system utilizes a disciplined **Soft (`1`)** approach (base radius of `4px` / `0.25rem`, scaling to `8px` / `0.5rem` on cards, and `12px` / `0.75rem` on prominent hero displays). 

This restrained curvature reflects precision cutting of reams of paper, laboratory equipment packaging, and government stamp framing, steering clear of toy-like or overly rounded shapes to maintain educational authority.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Button:** `#C25608` solid fill, `#FFFFFF` text, `4px` border radius, padding `0.75rem 1.5rem`. Label font in medium/bold Space Grotesk. On hover: shifts to `#A84705` with subtle 1px downward inset.
- **Secondary / Institutional Action:** Navy `#0F172A` background with crisp white text, or pure white surface with `1.5px solid #0F172A` outline and `#0F172A` text.
- **Ghost Action:** Transparent background, `#0F172A` text, hover fills with `#F1F5F9`.

### Chips & Badges
- **Curriculum & Verification Badges (CBC / JSS / KRA Compliant):** `4px` border radius, `0.25rem 0.625rem` padding. Light azure or sage background (`#F0FDF4` / `#E0F2FE`), sharp `1px` matching border, dark tinted label text (`#0369A1` / `#15803D`) with Space Grotesk `label-sm` in all-caps.
- **Category Chips (Textbooks, Stationery, Labware, Printing):** White background, `1px solid #E2E8F0`, rounded `4px`. Active state applies ochre border `#C25608` and subtle warm tint `#FFFBEB`.

### Cards & Catalogs
- **Product & Supply Cards:** Clean white cards with `1px solid #E2E8F0`. High-clarity thumbnail area with subtle `#F8FAFC` recessed backdrop. Price displayed in bold `#0F172A`, VAT indicator in muted `#64748B`.
- **Branch Location Card:** Highlights branch name (Luanda, Kapsabet, Mumias, Nairobi), physical landmark indicator (e.g., "Opp. Muslim Primary", "Next to Bata"), quick-dial call action, and WhatsApp direct quotation trigger.

### Inputs & Tables
- **Search & Filter Inputs:** Ivory-white `#FFFFFF` background, `1px solid #CBD5E1`, `4px` radius. Focus ring: `2px solid #C25608` with zero blur offset.
- **Procurement & Price Tables:** Strict alternating rows (`#FFFFFF` and `#F8FAFC`). Crisp hairline horizontal separators (`#E2E8F0`). Bold, monospaced tabular numerals for prices and wholesale pack counts.
